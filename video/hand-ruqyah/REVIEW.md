# REVIEW — رقية اليد على موضع الألم

Render: `../ruqyah-yad-ala-mawdi-alalam.mp4`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 52.7 s, delivery quality.
`hyperframes check`: passes with no warnings. HyperFrames CLI pinned at 0.8.211.

## What the voice says (transcript, from Whisper small + checks)

1. تشعر بألم أو تظن أنك أُصبتَ بعين؟ — أول خطوة ليست أن تذهب إلى أحد
2. أوصى النبي ﷺ عثمانَ بنَ أبي العاص رضي الله عنه حين شكا وجعًا في جسده، كما في صحيح مسلم
3. ضَعْ يدك على موضع الألم، وقل ثلاثًا: بسم الله — ثم قل [سبع] مرات: أعوذ بعزة الله وقدرته من شر ما أجد وأحاذر
4. ولماذا ترقي نفسك بنفسك؟ لأنك حين تقف بين يدي الله متألمًا منكسرًا، تكون في أصدق حالات الافتقار إليه،
   وكلما ازداد العبد افتقارًا وانكسارًا كان أقرب إلى الإجابة، كما أن أقرب ما يكون العبد من ربه وهو ساجد،
   لأن السجود انكسار — ابدأ من اليوم بنفسك، وشارك هذا المقطع مع من يحتاجه

## Creative calls

- One colour arc carries the story: pain is warm amber, the name of God turns it gold, then emerald calm.
  The orb that is pain in scene 1 is the same orb under the hand in scene 3 and the flood in scene 4.
- Seven scenes, 6–9 s each, a new beat every 1–3 s inside; transitions with a cause: whip, blur swap,
  colour flood from the orb, the bead ring growing through the frame, a light flare out of the sujūd.
- No music: ruqyah content. Voice, thumps on the pain beats and the seal, whooshes on the big moves,
  a crack when the vessel breaks, one noise riser into the sujūd touch.
- No people, faces or animals. The hand is line art, the eye an outline, the door a rectangle.

## Measured vs. looked at

- Timing: voice pauses cut it into 18 chunks matched 1:1 to the transcript. Words inside a chunk are
  spread by letter count, so a word can be off by up to ~0.15 s.
- Looked at: every scene in snapshots (about 60 frames), the seams and the fixed moments again.
- Sound: measured on the render, not heard.

## What I added that the voice does not say (check these)

- «٢٢٠٢» on the seal: the Sahih Muslim number I remember for this hadith. Verify before publishing.
- The Persian meaning under the dua: my translation of «أعوذ بعزة الله وقدرته من شر ما أجد وأحاذر».
- The labels «العبد» and «ربِّه» in scene 6 are names on a diagram, not a drawing of anything.

## Fixed in review

- Dua lines collided (diacritics) → bigger line gap, ring moved up and smaller.
- The chip covered the fingertips, the hand was a black glove → chip moved, hand fill 45 %.
- GSAP's SVG transform origin displaced the light, rays, star and flame → SVG shapes now animate only
  through opacity and attributes; anything that scales is an HTML box.
- Ripple rings showed before their time (hollow ring at 36–41 s) → `immediateRender: false`.
- Scene 6 was empty from 39.6 to 41.4 s → ground line and beam arrive with «كما أن».
- «ساجد» touched the equation chips; the share arrow overlapped the lamp; the gold flood lasted 0.9 s
  too long; the ring's faded beads crossed the Persian line at 25.9 s.

## Weakest shot, and what next

- 29–30 s: the mihrab arch is still drawing and the frame is quiet for ~1.5 s. A slow push-in or the
  stars brightening would carry it.
- The hand is a simple outline. A second pose (palm pressing, fingers spread) would feel more alive.
- Words inside long chunks (the dua, the sujūd line) are time-weighted, not transcribed word by word;
  a word-level transcript would tighten them.
